## What this is

You are being asked to appear briefly on camera as part of an evaluation
dataset for a face-recognition and person re-identification system built as
an academic project. You will be filmed walking naturally past two cameras
for a few seconds each. Some participants will be asked to do a second walk
wearing a different jacket or bag.

## What will be recorded and how it's used

- Short video clips (a few seconds each) of you walking past two camera
  positions.
- Frames extracted from those clips will be manually labelled with an
  identity tag (e.g. "Person 7") by the researcher, **not** your name, so
  that the system's output can be checked against ground truth.
- The footage and derived data (frames, face crops, labels) will be used
  **only** to test the accuracy of the identification system for this
  academic project — coursework, thesis, or research report. It will not be
  used for any commercial purpose, published publicly, or shared outside the
  project team without asking you again first.
